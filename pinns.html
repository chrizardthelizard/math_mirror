PINNs

Non-Regularized NN

Data regularization

Physics informed neural networks are a special case of regularization, so we will start by learning that concept. At it's most basic, regularization prevents overfitting. A model with many parameters can fit almost any dataset near perfectly, but this sort of naive approach can lead to a model that is unphysical. To paraphrase Enrico Fermi, with enough arbitrary parameters, you can fit an elephant, and with one more you can make it wiggle it's trunk12. We would like to avoid overfitting, thus we regularize.
